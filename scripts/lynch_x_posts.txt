Peter Lynch said: "Compare the price line to the earnings line. If earnings are growing but price isn't following, the stock is telling you something."

We ran his test on 33 sector leaders. Here's what the data showed 🧵


POST 1 — MSFT
─────────────
$MSFT in 12 months:

Price line: −23.2%
Earnings line: +32.8%

The gap between those two numbers is the Lynch signal. Stock is now trading at 22.9× earnings vs its 10-year median of 33.8× — a 32% discount to what the market has historically been willing to pay while the business kept compounding.

Price went on sale. Earnings didn't notice.

Lynch called these the stocks that make you money while you sleep.

That's P/E compression on a toll booth.
